Teaching

FSCI 6222 — Data Analysis (Analyse des données)

Graduate course · Université de Moncton · Spring–Summer 2026 · Lecturer (chargé de cours)

A hands-on introduction to data analysis with Python: NumPy and pandas, data cleaning and visualization, descriptive statistics, probability, correlation and regression, dimensionality reduction (PCA, t-SNE), and introductions to machine learning and deep learning. Each session paired slides with a Jupyter notebook on real datasets.

The course was taught in French. Slides are available in the original French (FR) and in an English translation (EN) with the same layout.
