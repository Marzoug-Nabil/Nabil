About Me

I am an AI researcher pursuing a thesis-based Master’s in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence) at Université de Moncton, where I also taught the graduate Data Analysis course. I hold an engineering degree in Data Science & AI from ENSA Safi and enjoy applying technology to real-world problems. I have a solid background in computer science, mathematics, and statistics, which allows me to approach projects with both analytical and practical perspectives.

My work involves developing and evaluating machine learning models, managing data, and ensuring reliable and efficient results. I value clarity, precision, and continuous learning in every task I undertake.

At the PRIME Lab my research focuses on generative AI for medical imaging (VAEs, GANs and hybrid VAE-GANs for segmentation and synthetic data) and on quantum machine learning for medical image classification.
