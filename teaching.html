FSCI 6222 — Data AnalysisAnalyse des données

Université de Moncton, Canada  ·  Lecturer (Chargé de cours)  ·  Taught in French

A hands-on introduction to data analysis with Python: from NumPy and pandas, cleaning and visualization, through descriptive statistics, probability and regression, to dimensionality reduction, machine learning and deep learning. Each session pairs slides with a Jupyter notebook on real datasets.
